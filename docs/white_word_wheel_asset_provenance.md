# White Word Wheel hamster asset provenance

The `themes/assets/white-word-wheel/hamster-wheel.gif` asset is a resized 480 × 480 GIF derived from the online **Hamster Running on a Wheel** animation published by Ignacio Alcaino Rodriguez on [LottieFiles](https://lottiefiles.com/free-animation/hamster-running-on-a-wheel-nDxt8flAq4). The source describes the work as an “Isometric hamster running on a wheel,” lists GIF among its available export formats, and states that it is free to use under the Lottie Simple License.

The asset was resized without cropping from 640 × 640 to 480 × 480 so that it remains within LockPlus 15’s bounded animated-GIF decoding limits. All 24 frames, 100 ms frame timing, white background, and infinite loop were preserved. The copied asset is stored in this repository for Theme Manager’s on-demand download flow and is not bundled inside the Debian package.
