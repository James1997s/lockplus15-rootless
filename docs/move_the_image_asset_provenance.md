For the revised wooden-frame layout, the user-requested animation was cropped to the landscape 268 × 220 frame dimensions and re-encoded as a fourteen-frame looping GIF. The crop preserves the visible red balloon and figure from the supplied animation and remains below the SpecialLock remote asset limits. The asset is fetched on demand after theme selection and is not bundled inside the Debian package.
